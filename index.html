Hafzan Network Impact Framework (HNIF)

A Network-Based Model for Predicting Market Impact in Fragmented Markets Using Venue-Level Dynamics

Academic Abstract

In increasingly fragmented financial markets, the estimation of trade-induced price impact is no longer a venue-isolated problem. This paper proposes the Hafzan Network Impact Framework (HNIF)—a model that integrates trade size, depth, and volatility with cross-venue correlations and eigenvector centrality. The framework captures non-linear impact behavior while accounting for the structural role of each venue in the liquidity network, offering substantial relevance to smart order routing and systemic risk monitoring in multi-venue environments.

Keywords: Market Impact Modeling, Multi-Venue Trading, HNIF, Network Theory, Financial Microstructure.

Variable Nomenclature
TSi: Trade Size — The volume of the order executed at trading venue i.
Di: Market Depth — The available liquidity or volume at the best bid/ask levels at venue i.
σi: Volatility — The standard deviation of asset returns (risk regime) at venue i.
β: Impact Exponent — A non-linear factor (typically > 1.0) modeling the power-law decay of liquidity.
Ci: Eigenvector Centrality — The structural importance of venue i based on its influence over other venues in the correlation network.
Corrij: Correlation — The statistical relationship between returns at the source venue i and the target venue j.
